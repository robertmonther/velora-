## Long description (both stores)

Velora is one catalogue of original songs, played whole.

Listen
- Every song from start to finish, never a clip.
- Lyrics that move with the music. Tap a line and the song jumps there.
- Jump to a song's parts: intro, verse, chorus.
- Genres, moods and collections: Pop, R&B / Soul, Romantic, Arena Anthems, Slowed and more.

Radio
- One station that never stops. Everyone hears the same song at the same moment.
- A different mood for each part of the day.

Yours
- Share a song as a card, or pick the lyric lines you want to show.
- Hide explicit songs on your device with one switch.
- No account needed to listen.

About the music
The lyrics, concepts and song design are written by the catalogue's creator. The music and voices are made with AI
(Suno). Every song says so in its credits.

## What to tell the stores (honest answers)

- **Category:** Music.
- **Age rating:** some songs have explicit lyrics and sensual themes. They carry an "E" mark and can be hidden. Answer
  the questionnaires with "mature / suggestive themes" and "explicit language in music". Expected: 16+ or 18+.
- **AI-generated content:** the music is AI-assisted, and it is the catalogue's own content, not made by users in
  the app. Google asks for a way to report AI content: the "Content and reports" page in the app has the address.
- **Accounts:** optional. An account can be deleted inside the app (Account, "Delete my account").
- **Purchases:** none in the app.
- **Data collected:** for listeners, nothing. With an account: email, name. No tracking, no ads identifiers.
- **Review notes (Apple):** "Listening needs no account. The Upload button is for the catalogue's owner; other
  accounts are listeners. No purchases are offered in the app."
